02 ABOUT

We started making movies before we knew how to make movies.

Flop Films is an independent filmmaking collective founded by Jayden Benjamin.

It started years before the name did — making ridiculous movies as kids, grabbing whatever camera was around, creating characters, and figuring everything out as we went.

Now we're doing the same thing. Just slightly better.
